The company

DMart is a chain of grocery stores across India. Every night, stores send sales files. Category managers want to know what sold, and where stock is thin. Finance hates a surprise Snowflake bill. Compliance wants loyalty emails and phone numbers locked. You are the person who makes that platform real. The database is DMART.

The cast

  • Dev Okonkwo — senior engineer. Never dumps jargon; always says why.
  • Kai Rivera — category analyst. Asks for numbers, and sometimes asks for the wrong object.
  • Riley Okada — pipelines. Files, pipes, and tasks.

  • Morgan Hale — store operations. Go-live, and the credit bill.
